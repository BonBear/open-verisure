## ⚡ Hardware & RF (CC1110 / CC1101 / 868 MHz)
- [2026-10-22] [CC1110/RF]: The RF core runs at 868.950 MHz with a 27 MHz crystal (X1). Register calculations for frequency and baud rate must be based on 27 MHz, not the standard TI 24 MHz or 26 MHz.
- [2026-10-22] [CC1110/DMA]: The RFD FIFO must be handled via DMA trigger 19 to guarantee that no bytes are dropped at high transmission rates.
- [2026-10-22] [ESP32-S2/Coexistence]: Use only ADC1-domain pins for the SPI bus to the CC1101. Using ADC2 pins creates high-frequency switching noise that knocks out the ESP32-S2's Wi-Fi receiver at 2.4 GHz.
- [2026-10-22] [SENSOR/GPIO]: The reed switch is on P1_7, Shock/Vibration on P1_2, and Tamper on P0_1.
- [2026-10-24] [MD_CAM/FLASHING]: The programming header on the GW MD CAM2 (SECUR-PCAM-BLACKFIN) is J6: J6-2=DD (P2_1/Pin 15), J6-3=DC (P2_2/Pin 16), J6-4=RESET_N (Pin 31/SW1 via 2.7 kOhm), J6-5=GND. Do NOT connect 3V3 from the ESP_CC_Flasher to VBAT (the inrush current into the tantalum capacitors C94/C50/C3 pulls down the ESP32 LDO and drops USB). Instead, power the board from 1 battery in the holder (~3.1V) and connect only 4 wires (GND, DD, DC, RST).
- [2026-10-24] [MD_CAM/RF]: The GW MD CAM2 (Board #1 = 2NCEKS3T unlocked, Board #2 = 2NCEKT3X locked reference) uses a 27.000 MHz crystal (Y1) and authenticates directly with the Gateway using FREQ=0x202EEF, MDMCFG4=0x8A, MDMCFG3=0x75 and FSCAL0=0x1F (RSSI -43 dBm, LQI 103). Set is_por_reset on both POR (rst_cause=0) and External Reset (rst_cause=1) so that the Gateway's replay filter is reset immediately after reflashing/reset. (2026-11-17: camera/old format only; the door sensor sends a persistent counter and the gateway has no reset exception for it, see GATEWAY/REPLAY_COUNTER)
- [2026-10-24] [MD_CAM/GPIO]: The PIR ALARM signal is on P1_7 (Pin 32, active low: 3.08V when idle, 0V on motion). Only SW3 is fitted on the board and it is detected via dual pull-up/pull-down scanning on P0 (mask 0xC1). When the Blackfin DSP's 3.3V rail is switched off (0V), P0INP=0x30 and P1INP=0x3F MUST be set (tristate on P0_4..P0_5 and P1_0..P1_5), otherwise the CC1110's internal pull-up leaks into the DSP's ESD diodes (~0.6V) and causes noise/false alarms.
- [2026-10-23] [MD_CAM/BUS]: The CC1110 talks to the Blackfin DSP (ADSP-BF512) and peripheral chips via SPI USART0 Alt. 2 (P1_3=SCK, P1_4=MISO, P1_5=MOSI, P1_2=SS1, P1_1=SS2) and UART USART1 Alt. 1 (P0_4=TX, P0_5=RX). The 3.3V, 2.5V, 1.8V and 1.2V rails are completely off (0V) in idle mode, while the CC1110 and the PIR are powered directly from VBAT (~3.1V).
- [2026-10-24] [CC1110/PM2]: The Port 1 edge is selected with PICTL bit 1 (P1ICON = 0x02), NOT 0x04. The wrong bit made the PIR wake only on the wrong edge. PM2 requires the CPU to be switched to the HS RCOSC first (CLKCON.OSC=1) before PCON.IDLE is set; every ISR must clear SLEEP.MODE and PxIFG before PxIF.
- [2026-10-24] [MD_CAM/PM2]: Working low-power design: the PIR (P1_7) wakes directly via the Port 1 interrupt; the Sleep Timer (WOR_RES=2, WOREVT0=16 = 0.5 s) wakes to read SW3 via a dual pull scan against the boot baseline (the battery is always inserted with the unit off the wall = Tamper) and to count down the heartbeat (7200 ticks = 1 h). The 27 MHz XOSC + radio are only switched on during transmission.
- [2026-10-29] [MD_CAM/TAMPER]: The tamper switch SW3 is on P1_6 (Pin 33), NOT P0_6! It closes to VBAT+. With Port 1 in pull-down, P1_6 reads 0 (0V) when the cover is off (Tamper Alarm), and P1_6=1 (3.0V) when the button is pressed against the wall (Cover Secure). P1_6 was previously thought to be a DSP control pin, which caused the internal pull-up to put 3V on the pin.
- [2026-10-28] [MD_CAM/POWER]: The Blackfin DSP (ADSP-BF512) power supply is controlled by the CC1110: P0_2=1 enables the 1.2V core via Q13, P1_0=1 enables the 3.3V switched rail via Q7, and P0_7=1 enables the auxiliary rail (J2-4). P0_0 is the active-low hardware reset (/RESET). With P0_0=0 the board draws ~20 mA (or 1 mA with all rails off). With P0_0=1 and the rails active, the Blackfin draws a stable 92-95 mA while actively running. (CONTRADICTED 2026-11-11: Q7/P1_0 holds the Blackfin /RESET and P0_0 is the supply, see Q7_RESET)
- [2026-10-28] [MD_CAM/FLASH]: U3 SPI Flash (marked "DSP V175") is an EON cFeon EN25F40 4 Mbit (512 KB) flash memory with JEDEC ID 0x1C 0x31 0x13. Its Chip Select (/CS, Pin 1) is controlled directly by the Blackfin's internal SPI0_SEL1 (not by the CC1110's SS pins). When the Blackfin is held in reset (P0_0=0), U3 /CS is pulled low and the CC1110 can read the flash via the shared SPI bus (SCK=P1_3, MISO=P1_4, MOSI=P1_5). When the Blackfin runs (P0_0=1) it drives SPI0_SEL1 high so that the U3 Flash is tri-stated in standby. (CONTRADICTED 2026-10-30, see U3_CS_IS_P1_1)
- [2026-10-28] [MD_CAM/SPI_HANDSHAKE]: The test pads SS Upper (P1_2) and SS Lower (P1_1) are inter-processor control lines connected directly to the Blackfin DSP (/SPISS). At startup the CC1110 must tri-state P1_2..P1_5 so that the Blackfin can boot undisturbed from the U3 Flash. After boot the CC1110 takes over as SPI Master. The init command 0x04 on P1_2 gives an immediate response on MISO (clamped from 0xFF to 0x00). (CONTRADICTED: the Blackfin boots from the EN25F20 and P1_2 enables the SPI1 path, see the later entries)
- [2026-10-28] [MD_CAM/BUS_SWITCH]: P1_1 works as an active-high Bus Switch Enable. When P1_1 = 1 the bus is fully open to the Blackfin and the CC1110 can clock 4096 clocks continuously. When P1_1 = 0 the bus is clamped low. (CONTRADICTED 2026-10-30, see U3_CS_IS_P1_1)
- [2026-10-28] [MD_CAM/BLACKFIN_SPI]: The Blackfin ADSP-BF512 runs stably at 73–97 mA (P0_0 = 1). Its SPI slave hardware is active and drives MISO to 0x00 (SENDZ = 1) during the entire 512-byte clock-out while waiting for the DSP application to load the transmit buffer. U3 Pin 2 rings out directly (0 ohm) to CC1110 Pin 35 (MISO).
- [2026-10-28] [MD_CAM/FLASH_LED]: The LED flash board (P/N3302042 C) has a red indicator LED that lights up when the board is powered. The flash LEDs (4 IR/high-power LEDs) are controlled via combined logic (Blackfin/P0_7/light sensor).
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/POWER_SEQ]: The ADSP-BF512 strictly requires VDDEXT (3.3V switched via P1_0/Q7) before VDDINT (1.2V core via P0_2/Q13) according to Analog Devices EE-274. Reversed power sequencing locks up the DSP's internal Power-On Reset/PLL. (CONTRADICTED 2026-11-11: see Q7_RESET)
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/BUS_SWITCH]: P1_1 is an active-high Bus Switch Enable. When P1_1 = 0 or floating in tristate, the bus is physically isolated and all lines (MOSI/MISO/SS/SCK) are clamped to 0V. For the bus to be connected and measurable, P1_1 must be actively driven HIGH (1). (CONTRADICTED 2026-10-30, see U3_CS_IS_P1_1)
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/DEBUG_HALT]: On "Detect CC" (/cc_init) the CC1110 enters Debug Halt and all I/O pins revert to internal pull-ups. This pulls P0_7 high, which turns on transistor Q2 and lights the red LED LD1 steadily. On "Reset / RunCC" (/cc_exit_dbg) the firmware's io_init() executes, actively grounds P0_7 to 0V and turns the LED off immediately (3.0 mA idle current).

## 🔐 Crypto, Security & Protocol
- [2026-10-22] [AES/KDF]: The CC1110 uses its built-in hardware coprocessor for AES-128. The device key is derived via a KDF based on the device's serial number and the master secret.
- [2026-10-22] [REPLAY]: Replay protection is based on a 16-bit rolling sequence counter. The Gateway must discard all packets whose counter is not strictly incrementing (taking wrap-around at 0xFFFF into account).
- [2026-10-22] [PAYLOAD]: The telemetry packet is 24 bytes long (includes battery level VDD/3, contact state and rolling sequence).

## 🛠️ Build System, Toolchains & Python
- [2026-10-22] [SDCC/8051]: The CC1110 is compiled with SDCC. Avoid dynamic allocation (`malloc`) entirely because of the 8051's limited XDATA/IDATA RAM.
- [2026-10-22] [PlatformIO]: The ESP32-S2 Gateway is compiled with PlatformIO. Make sure not to disturb the captive portal and MQTT tasks in FreeRTOS.
- [2026-10-22] [Python-tools]: The serial port selector is centralized in `port_selector.py` and must always be reused by other scripts instead of hardcoding COM ports.

## 📝 New Lessons Learned (continuously added by the agents)
- [2026-10-29] [MD_CAM/CAMERA_PIPELINE]: Complete picture transfer over 868.950 MHz Sub-1GHz RF to Home Assistant verified. The CC1110 segments the JPEG picture into 8-byte encrypted frames (plain[2]=0x80), the ESP32-S2 Gateway reassembles the stream, validates the JPEG SOI (0xFF 0xD8) and EOI (0xFF 0xD9), and publishes via MQTT to `camera.verisure_2nceks3t_camera`.
- [2026-10-29] [SDCC/MEMORY_MODELS]: In SDCC for the 8051 (--model-large), `__code` pointers (ROM) must not be passed directly to transmit functions that use `MOVX` for XDATA without explicitly copying into an `__xdata` buffer. Reading beyond XDATA into the SFR address space (0xF000+) hangs the 8051 bus.
- [2026-10-29] [CC1110/UART_ISR]: On the CC1110, `U1DBUF` must always be assigned to a `volatile uint8_t` in the USART1 RX ISR (`urx1_isr`). Statements like `(void)U1DBUF;` are optimized away by SDCC, so the RX interrupt flag is never cleared by the hardware, which causes an endless interrupt storm.
- [2026-10-29] [MD_CAM/SHUTTER_BUTTON]: The tamper switch SW3 (P1_6) works excellently as a physical camera shutter button. When the button is pressed (P1_6=1, 3.0V), a trigger packet (0xEE) is sent and the picture sequence is fired with immediate RF delivery to Home Assistant.
- [2026-10-29] [MD_CAM/PIR_INDICATOR_LED]: The red indicator LED LD1 is on the PIR daughterboard (SECUR-PCAM BF-PIR P/N 3309012 A) and is driven by transistor Q2. If unused pins (P0_1, P0_3, P0_6, P2_0) are left as default inputs with pull-up, Q2 is kept on and draws ~19 mA extra (22 mA total). By actively configuring these pins and driving them low (0.0 V), LD1 turns off and the current consumption drops immediately to 3 mA when idle.
- [2026-10-29] [MD_CAM/P1_PULLUP]: Port 1 contains both the PIR sensor (P1_7) and the tamper switch SW3 (P1_6). Since the CC1110 has a common pull direction for the whole of Port 1 (P2INP bit 6, PDUP1), Port 1 must NEVER be set to pull-down during operation, as that pulls P1_7 to 0V and causes continuous false PIR alarms. Port 1 must always be kept in pull-UP mode (P2INP &= ~0x40).
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/BOOT_CAPTURE_VERIFIED]: Measurements on Reference Board #2 (locked) with a Saleae Logic 4 captured a complete 2.000 MHz SPI boot sequence to the U3 Flash (EN25F40) at t=42.286s (opcodes 0x05 RDSR, 0x9F RDID, 0x0B Fast Read at 0x000000, 0x020000, 0x040000, 0x07F000, 0x07E200, 0x07E000). The total boot duration is exactly 20.7 ms, after which U3 /CS returns to HIGH (Standby) and the Blackfin switches its internal SPI0 controller from SPI Master to SPI Slave.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/BLACKFIN_BOOT_FIX]: For the Blackfin ADSP-BF512 to be able to boot from the U3 Flash on Board #1 (where the rails are controlled via CC1110 Q13/Q7 instead of a simultaneous cold start): P1_1 (Bus Switch Enable) MUST be actively driven HIGH (1) and P1_2 (Blackfin /SPISS) MUST be held HIGH (1) during the boot phase to prevent an SPI Mode Fault in the DSP, while P1_3..P1_5 (SCK, MISO, MOSI) are kept in true 3-state (P1INP |= 0x38). (CONTRADICTED: the Blackfin boots from the EN25F20, not U3, see the later entries)
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/POWER_CURRENTS]: Verified current levels on the bench power supply (COM5, 3.00 V): idle with LD1 off draws exactly 3.0 mA. During active Blackfin boot and exposure the board draws 73.0–75.0 mA for ~1.5 seconds and then deterministically returns to 3.0 mA.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/UART_TELEMETRY]: Telemetry opcode 0xDD implemented in the CC1110 to report the number of bytes received from the Blackfin on UART1 (P0_5/RX) over Sub-1GHz RF to the ESP32-S2 Gateway.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/SPI_UNCLAMP_DISCOVERY]: The Blackfin SPI slave hardware drives SCK, MISO, MOSI low (0, 0, 0 = 0xC5 on P1) if it starts with P1_2 = 1. By holding P1_2 = 0 during reset release / power ramp-up and then releasing it to 1, the Blackfin releases the bus to full High-Z (0xFD on P1 with pull-ups). In this unclamped state the U3 NOR Flash can be read without errors (0x1C 0x31 0x13) via P1_1 = 1 even while the Blackfin is actively running (71–75 mA), and it is silenced completely (0xFF 0xFF 0xFF) with P1_1 = 0.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/FLASHER_OPTIMIZATION]: In build_and_flash_md_cam.py the binary is uploaded to the flasher before cc_init is called, which minimizes the time in debug halt and eliminates unnecessary current consumption and lighting of the indicator LED LD1.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/CAMERA_STATUS]: No real camera picture has been received so far. The only JPEG in scratch (`u3_dump_live.bin`, 5464 B) is byte for byte the test picture in `camera_image.h`. The U3 dumps that show `1C 31 13` repeated (or the same ID bit-shifted, `C4 4C 70`) mean that the flash was stuck in RDID mode, because the CC1110 cannot toggle U3 /CS.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/BLACKFIN_NO_BOOT]: Saleae measurement at cold start (12 MS/s) on Board #1: neither with P1_1=0/P1_2=0 nor with P1_1=1/P1_2=1 (P1_3..5 without pull) at reset release is any 2 MHz boot read (05/9F/0B) to U3 visible, despite 73–97 mA. SCK/MOSI/MISO are pulled low about 57 µs after the 3.3V rail (P1_0) is switched on and stay low. Reference Board #2, on the other hand, does 05 → 9F → 0B 07F000/07E200/07E000/000000/020000/040000 about 691 ms after power-on.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/U3_CS_IS_P1_1]: Saleae with the probe directly on U3 pin 1 (/CS), Board #1: U3 /CS follows P1_1 inverted (P1_1=1 → /CS low, P1_1=0 → /CS high), on all 5 writes of P1_1. With P1_1=0 and the Blackfin rails off, /CS is high. P1_1 is therefore the CC1110's chip select for U3, not a bus switch, and /CS is not controlled by the Blackfin's SPI0_SEL1.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/BLACKFIN_NOT_SPI_MASTER]: During 1.58 s with /CS low, the rails on and reset released, the Blackfin never clocks SCK on U3 pin 6 (measured directly on U3). SCK/MOSI/MISO are pulled low about 53 µs after P1_1/P1_0 are switched on and stay that way.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/U3_DUMP_METHOD]: U3 can be read reliably from the CC1110 with the Blackfin in reset (P0_0=0), P1_2=1, P1_0=1 and then P0_2=1, USART0 SPI Mode 0 (353 kHz) and P1_1 as /CS around each command (P1_1=1 select, P1_1=0 deselect). JEDEC 1C 31 13 was confirmed before each block, and four separate runs gave identical data (the routine `dump_u3_rf()` in main.c, 256 B/block over RF ≈ 4 s/block).
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/U3_CONTENT_BOARD1]: Board #1's U3 (all 512 KB scanned, 2048 pages of 256 B) contains no Blackfin firmware. Only three pages are written, the rest is 0xFF: 0x07E000 (the first 135 bytes = 0x00), 0x07E200 (1 byte 0x00) and 0x07F000 (3 bytes 0x00). These are exactly the configuration addresses that Reference Board #2 reads at boot before it reads 0x000000/0x020000/0x040000. The camera on Board #1 cannot work until a Blackfin image is written to U3, e.g. a copy of Reference Board #2's U3 read with a SOIC-8 clip.
- [2026-10-30] [MD_CAM/U5_BLACKFIN]: U5 is marked ADSP-BF512 KBCZ-4 (3402805.1 0.2, #1605), i.e. the variant WITHOUT F and without internal flash memory. The Blackfin must get its program from outside: from U3 or loaded by the CC1110. Photo: MD_CAM/docs/images/blackfin_bf512_chip.jpeg.

The same three pages are written: 0x07E000 (135 B 0x00, identical to Board #1), 0x07E200 (3 B 0x00, Board #1 has 1 B) and 0x07F000 (3 B 0x00). U3 is therefore configuration or picture memory. - [2026-10-31] [MD_CAM/BOARD2_WAKE_CYCLE]: Reference Board #2 (original firmware, unpaired) reads U3 (JEDEC 1C 31 13 + config pages) and writes ":OK; \n" on UART TX (P0_4, 115200 8N1) in a fixed pattern every ~0.8 s and ~2.5 s.
