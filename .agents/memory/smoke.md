# Memory: Smoke Detector GW-SD3 (EFR32FG14P231, W25Q16JL, IC81, IC301, CP92)

## Smoke Detector (`GW-SD3`)
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/POWER_AND_PADS]: Powered by `1× CR-2/3AZ` (`3.0 V` Li-MnO2). Back PCB power pads: `01`/`03`/`29` = `BAT+`, `02`/`04`/`27` = `GND`, `VCC` = `3.0 V` always-on, `VCA` = `0 V` in idle. `R1`+`R56` = 2× 27 Ω parallel (13.5 Ω to `BAT+`).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/SWD_AAP]: Back pads `CLK` = EFR32 pin 1 (`PF0`), `DIO` = pin 2 (`PF1`), `RES` = pin 12 (`RESETn`), `SW` = pin 25 (`PA0`, not SWO). Raspberry Pi Debug Probe (`D` port: Orange=`CLK`, Yellow=`DIO`, Black=`GND`) + `pyOCD` (`DebugPort.connect()`, 200 kHz, 3.0 V) cleanly reads `DPIDR = 0x2BA01477` and `AP 0 IDR = 0x26E60011` (Silicon Labs Series 1 AAP = debug-locked, recoverable via `AAP DEVICE_ERASE`).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/SPI_FLASH_IC6]: `IC6` (`W25Q16JL`, 16 Mbit, 2.3–3.6 V) is power-gated (`0 V` in idle) and fully exposed on back PCB pads: `54` = `/CS`, `51` = `DO` (MISO), `53` = `DI` (MOSI), `52` = `CLK`, `60` = `VCC`+`/WP`+`/HOLD`, `02`/`04`/`27` = `GND` (no SOIC-8 clip needed).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/PERIPHERALS]: `CN301` (`M36BN20-A` 8 Ω 0.5 W speaker) connects via back pads `43`/`42` to `IC301` (`304A`) pins 13+14 and 15+16 (BTL speaker driver). Optical chamber: see [SMOKE/IC81_PINS] (the earlier `LED81` → `PF4` note is superseded). `CP92` (DFN-8 with open sensing window) is a digital temperature & humidity sensor.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/RF_868]: Stock GW-SD3 boot frames confirmed on OpenVerisure gateway `CC1101#1` (`868.950 MHz`, 2-FSK, 38.4 kBaud, sync `D3 91`) at `-55 dBm` (LQI 51–57). `SiliconLabs/gecko_sdk` `v4.4.6` LTS provides `efr32fg14p231f{128,256}gm48.h` + `librail_efr32xg14_gcc_release.a` for `arm-none-eabi-gcc`.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/SPI_FLASH_IC6]: In-circuit dump works with the board at 3.0 V on `BAT+`, `RES` tied to `GND` and 3.0 V fed directly to `IC6` pin 8 (stays 0 V otherwise while the EFR32 is in reset); FT232H `AD0`-`AD3` = CLK/DI/DO//CS, JEDEC `EF 40 15`. Without `RES` low the EFR32 runs (~223 mA load-test pulses, see [SMOKE/LOAD_TEST]) and corrupts the bus; compare at least two reads.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/IC6_CONTENTS]: `IC6` holds only sound data: 16 × 128-byte sequence records at `0x0` (big-endian `(start,end)` pairs, top byte flags `0x80` tone/silence, `0x12`/`0x32` clip), 19 clips up to `0x17AFFF`, tone pattern to `0x183FFF`, rest erased. No firmware/GBL/vector table, so the stock application exists only in the locked EFR32 and is lost on AAP unlock. Clips are not IMA ADPCM.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/STOCK_SNIFF]: Stock firmware traces: `PB11` (EFR32 pin 31) → `IC301` pin 21 is the audio-IC control line (high ~13 s after power-on, ~1.1 s on test press, else 10.6 µs pulse every 4 s); speaker line idles at 0 V and only steps to VCC for ~30 ms on test (no tone without speaker); `IC6` CLK stays idle during boot/test. `CP92` = power-gated via load switch on `PF2`, single data line on `PF3` (not I2C). Verify every Saleae clip by tapping `BAT+` first: loose clips gave flat captures.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/AAP_UNLOCK]: Unit 1 (`3EB7KFZQ`; serials were swapped in notes before 2026-11-27) unlocked via AAP over pyOCD (`AAP_CMDKEY` = `0xCFACC118`, `AAP_CMD` bit0 `DEVICEERASE`, then bit1 `SYSRESETREQ`); erase took ~0.1 s, AP0 then reads AHB-AP `0x24770011`. Stock application is gone on this unit; `3EA8VKXK` and `3EA8W6D6` stay stock. Otii Arc 3.0 V / 0.5 A limit (board peaks ~330 mA).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/DEVINFO]: Unit 1 DEVINFO: `MSIZE` `0x00200100` = 256 kB flash / 32 kB SRAM, `PART` `0x1D3D00E7` = family 61 / device 231, i.e. `EFR32FG14P231F256GM48` (use `efr32fg14p231f256gm48.h`); CPUID `0x410FC241` (Cortex-M4F r0p1). Main flash and lock-bits page blank after erase.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/RF_TX]: Unit 1 firmware sends door-format packets (built with ID `3EA8VKXK` by mistake until 2026-11-27, now `3EB7KFZQ`; stock units are `3EA8VKXK` and `3EA8W6D6`) that the release gateway authenticates and publishes on MQTT every 10 s at -45 dBm, freq. error -30 kHz before the CTUNE trim (see [SMOKE/HFXO_CTUNE]). EFR32 PHY must use sync `D391D391` (32 bits, CC11xx 30/32 mode sends the word twice), MSB first, 32-bit `AA` preamble, fixed 26 bytes with software CRC-16. With the ground screw of the wire antenna removed the board reached the gateway only at ~-75 dBm CW and no packet was received: check the antenna before blaming the PHY.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/DEBUG]: pyOCD's default connect resets the EFR32 (counters read as freshly booted); pass `connect_mode=attach` in `options` to peek a running target. A pyOCD disconnect clears `DEMCR.TRCENA`, so a DWT-cycle-counter delay hangs after flashing; time with `RAIL_GetTime()` instead.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/BATTERY_EM2]: ADC0 `posSel=adcPosSelAVDD` with `adcRef5V` (mV = code * 5000 / 4095) reads 2964-2966 mV on Unit 1 at Otii 3.0 V. EM2 between packets (RTCC on LFXO via RAIL_Sleep/RAIL_Wake, TIMERSYNC disabled) keeps the 10.0 s period, and pyOCD still flashes while the target sleeps. Sleep currents: see [SMOKE/SLEEP_CURRENT]; the old 222 mA load was PC6 floating high (see [SMOKE/PINS]).
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/SLEEP_CURRENT]: Unit 1 production firmware (no DC-DC init, pins_init) draws ~6.4 mA in EM0 and ~8 µA in EM2 at 3.0 V; stock 3EA8VKXK idles at ~2–3 µA (5–16 µA average, ~221 mA load-test pulse). A sticky bad state (EM0 ~23 mA, EM2 ~1.8 mA, EM4S 0.27 mA, DECOUPLE 2.67 V) survived short Otii off/on cycles and cleared only after the board sat unpowered: suspect latch-up, give a long power-off before trusting current numbers.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/PINS]: EM2 pull scan: PC6 high = battery load-test MOSFET on (~220 mA via R1/R56, the earlier constant 222 mA), PD13 high +7 mA, PD15 high +3 mA, PF2 low +0.35 mA (CP92 switch); firmware must hold PC6 low. VREGSW–DVDD (pins 38–40) reads 0.3 Ω (DC-DC inductor fitted) but EMU_DCDCINIT_DEFAULT raised EM2 to ~11 mA: leave the DC-DC off until the stock configuration is known.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/BATTERY_READ]: An AVDD ADC read within ~1 ms of EM2 wake is 40-120 mV low (2968 mV vs 3009 mV 5 ms later, codes stable within the burst = wake transient, not ADC settling). Read the battery after TX and send it with the next packet: stable 3008 mV at 3.0 V.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/HFXO_CTUNE]: Unit 1 carrier vs. gateway FREQEST: CTUNE 0x168 (reset default) -28.6 kHz, 0x0F0 +4.8 kHz, 0x100 0.0 kHz, 0x110 -4.8 kHz (~0.3 kHz/step, higher CTUNE = lower frequency); firmware default HFXO_CTUNE = 0x100. CTUNE can only be written with the HFXO off (emlib asserts), so trimming needs a rebuild per value.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/PACKET_TYPE]: Smoke packets carry type 0x5D (status) in plain[12], status bits in plain[13] (0x01 fault, 0x02 dirty, 0x04 test, 0x08 heartbeat, 0x10 hushed); 0x5E/0x5F reserved for CP92 temperature/humidity. The gateway classifies per packet (only for non-camera/siren IDs), so every smoke type must stay in 0x5D..0x5F; door firmware cannot emit them (LED bits 0x14 low when P0 is sampled). Bench: Unit 1 published as Smoke Detector with 11 HA entities.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/CHAMBER_SCAN]: `-DCHAMBER_SCAN` probe on Unit 1 (120 µs PC6 pulse, ADC on 24 pins, enables none/PD13/PD15/both): the `PC7` ADC row follows PC6 (see [SMOKE/LOAD_TEST]), AVDD sags ~65 mV during the pulse, and no other pin reacts to it. Floating mid-rail pins (PF5-7, PC9-11, PB11/12, PD14, PA3) only drift from ADC sampling. Externally high: PA0, PA4, PA5, PB13, PF2 pull; low: PA1, PA2, PC8, PF3. The IC81 output does not reach any GPIO in these states: map IC81 with a DMM before more probing.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/IC81_PINS]: `IC81` has 16 pins (not TSSOP-20). DMM on unpowered Unit 1: pin 14 = 1 Ω to EFR32 pin 33 (`PB13`, idles high = digital link), 18 kΩ to `PA1`, 13 kΩ to `PA2`; pins 9/10/11 = `PD81`; pin 15 VCC; pin 16 = 0 Ω to `LED81` (IC81 drives the IR LED; `LED81` is 12 kΩ to pin 14); pins 6/7/8/12/13 = `IC301` pins 1/2/4/28/29; pin 1 and 5 no link found; pins 2-4 ~100 kΩ to several nets. The other `LED81` leg goes through a diode to `BAT+` (IC81 pin 16 sinks the LED current). EFR32 reaches the chamber only via `PB13` (and `IC301` via `PB11`).
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/LOAD_TEST]: `PC6` (pin 43) drives a 3-pin SOT-23 N-MOSFET gate via 9 kΩ (part marking not read; `K2226` is the PCB code); source GND, drain to `R1`/`R56` (13.5 Ω to `BAT+`) = battery load test (~222 mA), not the IR LED. `PC7` (pin 44) reads as GND on the DMM, yet the chamber scan ADC (`APORT1YCH7`) saw it follow `PC6`: recheck the PC7 APORT mapping before trusting that row.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/STOCK_CHAMBER]: Stock `3EA8VKXK` Saleae (40 s, idle): `PB11` 10 µs pulse every 4.000 s; every second one (8 s period) is followed 6.4 ms later by a 2.54 ms high pulse on `IC81` pin 12 (from `IC301` pin 28), and ~2.1 ms into that window `IC81` pin 16 (`LED81` cathode) drops from ~2.18 V to ~1.25 V for ~90 µs = the IR LED flash. `PB13` (`IC81` pin 14) idles high; one capture showed a single 201 µs low pulse ~8.7 s after power-on. `IC301` therefore sequences the chamber (smoke ASIC) and `IC81` drives the LED/photodiode; how the result reaches the EFR32 is still open.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/STOCK_TEST_PRESS]: On stock `3EA8VKXK` the test needs a ~10 s button hold (short presses do nothing visible). With the speaker fitted (muffled, Otii 3.0 V / 0.5 A) it gave a short chirp while held and a longer quiet tone ~9 s later, no full-volume alarm; `IC81` pin 16 rises to ~2.4-2.75 V during both (speaker-drive crosstalk). `PB11`/`PB13`/`IC81` pin 12 showed no test edge (unlike the ~1.1 s `PB11` high seen on Unit 1 before unlock, [SMOKE/STOCK_SNIFF]); the 4 s / 8 s cycle paused ~43 s, then resumed. Two captures after short presses showed no cycle at all after power-on (unexplained: loose clips or a post-test pause). The IC301 -> EFR32 result line is still unmapped.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/TEST_BUTTON]: Test button = GND to EFR32 pin 25 (`PA0`, also the back `SW` pad) through 22 Ω; `PA0` idles high via an external pull-up (chamber scan), so the button is active low. Stock firmware needs a ~10 s hold to start its test.
- [2026-11-27] [SMOKE/IC301_PINS]: DMM on unpowered Unit 1: `IC301` is the SPI master of `IC6` (pin 5 = `DO`, 6 = `CLK`, 10 = `DI`, 11 = `/CS` 47 Ω, 32 = `GND`) and plays the clips itself. Pin 12 -> diode anode, cathode -> pad `32` -> debug pad `RES` = EFR32 `RESETn`; no resistor to `GND` (the 2 kΩ was a probe short), so `IC301` cannot reset the EFR32, only see `RES` pulled low. Pins 3, 25, 30, 31 read `GND` but need a recheck (probe short during that round); 13-16 speaker; 24 cap to `GND`; 17-23 beep through decoupling caps (19/20 VCC, 21 = `PB11`); 7 -> pad `47`, 9 -> pad `46`; 8 no link; 27/28 -> `IC81` 12/13 (earlier map said 28/29: recheck). The only signal link to the EFR32 is `PB11`.
