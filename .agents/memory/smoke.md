# Memory: Smoke Detector GW-SD3 (EFR32FG14P231, W25Q16JL, IC81, IC301, CP92)

## Smoke Detector (`GW-SD3`)
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/POWER_AND_PADS]: Powered by `1× CR-2/3AZ` (`3.0 V` Li-MnO2). Back PCB power pads: `01`/`03`/`29` = `BAT+`, `02`/`04`/`27` = `GND`, `VCC` = `3.0 V` always-on, `VCA` = `0 V` in idle. `R1`+`R56` = 2× 27 Ω parallel (13.5 Ω to `BAT+`).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/SWD_AAP]: Back pads `CLK` = EFR32 pin 1 (`PF0`), `DIO` = pin 2 (`PF1`), `RES` = pin 12 (`RESETn`), `SW` = pin 25 (`PA0`, not SWO). Raspberry Pi Debug Probe (`D` port: Orange=`CLK`, Yellow=`DIO`, Black=`GND`) + `pyOCD` (`DebugPort.connect()`, 200 kHz, 3.0 V) cleanly reads `DPIDR = 0x2BA01477` and `AP 0 IDR = 0x26E60011` (Silicon Labs Series 1 AAP = debug-locked, recoverable via `AAP DEVICE_ERASE`).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/SPI_FLASH_IC6]: `IC6` (`W25Q16JL`, 16 Mbit, 2.3–3.6 V) is power-gated (`0 V` in idle) and fully exposed on back PCB pads: `54` = `/CS`, `51` = `DO` (MISO), `53` = `DI` (MOSI), `52` = `CLK`, `60` = `VCC`+`/WP`+`/HOLD`, `02`/`04`/`27` = `GND` (no SOIC-8 clip needed).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/PERIPHERALS]: `CN301` (`M36BN20-A` 8 Ω 0.5 W speaker) connects via back pads `43`/`42` to `IC301` (`304A`) pins 13+14 and 15+16 (BTL speaker driver). Optical chamber: `LED81` = EFR32 pin 5 (`PF4`), `PD81` → `IC81` (`C4660`, optical AFE). `CP92` (DFN-8 with open sensing window) is a digital temperature & humidity sensor.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/RF_868]: Stock GW-SD3 boot frames confirmed on OpenVerisure gateway `CC1101#1` (`868.950 MHz`, 2-FSK, 38.4 kBaud, sync `D3 91`) at `-55 dBm` (LQI 51–57). `SiliconLabs/gecko_sdk` `v4.4.6` LTS provides `efr32fg14p231f{128,256}gm48.h` + `librail_efr32xg14_gcc_release.a` for `arm-none-eabi-gcc`.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/SPI_FLASH_IC6]: In-circuit dump works with the board at 3.0 V on `BAT+`, `RES` tied to `GND` and 3.0 V fed directly to `IC6` pin 8 (stays 0 V otherwise while the EFR32 is in reset); FT232H `AD0`-`AD3` = CLK/DI/DO//CS, JEDEC `EF 40 15`. Without `RES` low the EFR32 runs (~223 mA `LED81` pulses) and corrupts the bus; compare at least two reads.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/IC6_CONTENTS]: `IC6` holds only sound data: 16 × 128-byte sequence records at `0x0` (big-endian `(start,end)` pairs, top byte flags `0x80` tone/silence, `0x12`/`0x32` clip), 19 clips up to `0x17AFFF`, tone pattern to `0x183FFF`, rest erased. No firmware/GBL/vector table, so the stock application exists only in the locked EFR32 and is lost on AAP unlock. Clips are not IMA ADPCM.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/STOCK_SNIFF]: Stock firmware traces: `PB11` (EFR32 pin 31) → `IC301` pin 21 is the audio-IC control line (high ~13 s after power-on, ~1.1 s on test press, else 10.6 µs pulse every 4 s); speaker line idles at 0 V and only steps to VCC for ~30 ms on test (no tone without speaker); `IC6` CLK stays idle during boot/test. `CP92` = power-gated via load switch on `PF2`, single data line on `PF3` (not I2C). Verify every Saleae clip by tapping `BAT+` first: loose clips gave flat captures.
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/AAP_UNLOCK]: Unit 1 (`3EA8VKXK`) unlocked via AAP over pyOCD (`AAP_CMDKEY` = `0xCFACC118`, `AAP_CMD` bit0 `DEVICEERASE`, then bit1 `SYSRESETREQ`); erase took ~0.1 s, AP0 then reads AHB-AP `0x24770011`. Stock application is gone on this unit; `3EB7KFZQ` stays stock. Otii Arc 3.0 V / 0.5 A limit (board peaks ~330 mA).
- [2026-11-26] [SMOKE/DEVINFO]: Unit 1 DEVINFO: `MSIZE` `0x00200100` = 256 kB flash / 32 kB SRAM, `PART` `0x1D3D00E7` = family 61 / device 231, i.e. `EFR32FG14P231F256GM48` (use `efr32fg14p231f256gm48.h`); CPUID `0x410FC241` (Cortex-M4F r0p1). Main flash and lock-bits page blank after erase.
